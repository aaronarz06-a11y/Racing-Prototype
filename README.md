What went well?
The acceleration works well and was pretty improtant for me to get right, what I think turned out the best is the replacement for the wheels, I made a empty object that would change angles with A and D and placed it near the front of the player that way it could somewhat simulate two wheel drive.

What didn’t go well?
The movement and physics where really tricky to get down that they took up most of my time. First the player would start flying if they were in the air and players held A, then if the player drove over the seams of two cubes regardless of how smooth the transition was they would get sent up, then the player would just start falling through the floor. Additionally since the player was going fast the walls wouldn't block the player, although i did mitigate the problems they still cause issues.

What challenges did you face? (Technical, creative, or production)
One of the biggest challenges was replicating the drifitng system from Need for Speed Hot pursuit, in the game theres two ways to drift, handbrakes and reguar brake, I put in both in my prototype but they dont act very similar.

What can you do differently next time?
Next time I would look more into collision solutions and alternative movement since that ate up most of my time and is just generally useful for anything else.
